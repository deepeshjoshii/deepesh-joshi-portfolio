About

I wrote an initiating-coverage report on Juniper Green Energy, a 98-page dossier on the Indian battery industry, and built DCF, SOTP and scenario models for Exide, Amara Raja, Juniper and Wipro. Equity analysis tools: Beta Calculator and Reverse DCF

Python, Streamlit, quantitative finance

The Beta Calculator runs regression beta against a benchmark you choose, with Blume adjustment, stability checks, rolling beta, bottom-up peer beta and cost of equity. The Reverse DCF solves for the growth rate today's price already implies, using net income, FCFF or FCFE, and compares it with the company's own track record.
